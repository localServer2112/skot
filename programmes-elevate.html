About the programme

SKOT Elevate is the dedicated training arm of SKOT Communications, created to equip professionals, entrepreneurs and teams across Nigeria with the practical skills and strategic insight required to advance their careers, strengthen organisations and achieve measurable business outcomes. Organisations can access tailored workshops, virtual sessions, hybrid programmes, executive coaching, organisational training and retainer-based capacity development.

Under SKOT Elevate

Masterclass.

Masterclass sits under SKOT Elevate as a focused learning experience for junior, mid-level and senior communications professionals who want to refine their strategic communication capabilities and master modern public relations practice. Delivered as a quarterly deep dive, it explores contemporary PR, advanced media relations, digital PR, content creation, leadership communication and strategic influence.
