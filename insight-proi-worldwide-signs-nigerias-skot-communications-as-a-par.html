PROI Worldwide signs Nigeria’s SKOT Communications as a partner agency

Chicago, USA: PROI Worldwide, a global network of independent communications agencies announced its partnership with SKOT Communications, a strategic communications consultancy with a strong Sub- Saharan Africa focus based in Lagos, Nigeria, today. This collaboration strengthens the partnership’s position in the West African market.

Founded in November 2023 by Tòkunbòh George-Taylor, a highly accomplished communications professional with extensive experience as the pioneering MD of Hill+Knowlton Strategies Nigeria, SKOT Communications has quickly established itself as a prominent player in the Nigerian PR landscape.

The partnership with PROI Worldwide provides SKOT Communications with invaluable access to a global network of 90 agencies, expanding opportunities for collaboration and knowledge sharing. This alliance also enables SKOT to tap into new markets, enhancing its service offerings.

“We are excited to join the PROI Worldwide network,” said Tòkunbòh George-Taylor, Founder and CEO of SKOT Communications. “This partnership reflects our commitment to maintaining a global outlook and expanding our reach to better serve our clients. We believe that collaboration within PROI will enable our agency to deliver cutting-edge PR solutions while fostering the growth of our talented team.”

SKOT Communications’ key strengths include its dedication to global best practices and its team of highly qualified communications professionals. The agency’s expertise centers on consumer C brand communications; corporate C public affairs; media C digital strategy; and crisis and issues management. Key clients include major international organisations such as Netflix, Visa, and Informa Markets.

PROI’s Angela Scaffidi, the Global Chair PROI Worldwide, says SKOT is a right fit for PROI Worldwide. “Their clear offering, commitment to excellence and strong leadership align with ours and we are looking forward to the contributions they will make.”

About SKOT Communications: SKOT Communications is a strategic communications consultancy with a strong Sub-Saharan Africa focus. SKOT specialises in helping individuals, brands, companies and organisations discover and amplify their unique stories. Using its expertise, creativity and innovative ideas, it not only define, redefine, or amplify voices, it also creates real connections that grow relationships and loyalty and deliver measurable results.

About PROI Worldwide: PROI Worldwide harnesses the collective power of the world’s most entrepreneurial communications firms. With 90 partners in 65 countries and over 9,100 employees, PROI represents a combined revenue exceeding US$1.108 billion, establishing it as a leading force in the global communication industry.
