Case study

Bringing E1's global brand experience to the Lagos waterfront

The Oriental Hotel waterfront transformed into a flagship hospitality destination for the inaugural African edition of the E1 Grand Prix.

Client

E1 Series — Lagos Grand Prix

E1 Series — Lagos Grand Prix

Impact

Qualitative mandate — no quantified performance figures were provided in the source material.

The challenge

When Lagos hosted the inaugural African edition of the E1 Grand Prix, the Oriental Hotel served as one of the race's flagship hospitality destinations. The environment needed to reflect the energy and prestige of the global E1 property while creating a distinctive waterfront experience for guests, government representatives and international stakeholders. The task extended beyond venue branding: the physical setting and guest journey had to work as one coherent brand experience.

Our approach

SKOT developed and executed a premium experiential environment that transformed the Oriental Hotel waterfront into a hospitality and brand destination for the E1 Lagos GP weekend.

Large-format waterfront branding, viewing deck installations, hospitality lounges and environmental graphics brought E1's visual identity into the space. Guest arrival, seating, movement and hospitality touchpoints were coordinated to support a seamless experience.

Hospitality operations, curated guest gifting and vendor coordination were integrated into the activation, with physical elements aligned to E1's international visual identity standards.

The impact

The Oriental Hotel activation hosted senior government officials, international stakeholders, media representatives and VIP guests during race weekend. The venue provided an immersive waterfront setting aligned with E1's global brand identity, bringing spatial design, guest experience and hospitality coordination together within the wider Lagos Grand Prix programme.

The SKOT contribution

SKOT translated E1's international brand identity into a premium Lagos waterfront hospitality environment.
