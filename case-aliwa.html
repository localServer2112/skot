Case study

Establishing a new voice in Nigeria's leadership conversation

Launching the inaugural Nigeria Leadership Dialogue from a standing start — no established press relationships, a crowded conference landscape.

Client

Africa Leadership Initiative West Africa

Africa Leadership Initiative West Africa

The challenge

As Africa Leadership Initiative West Africa (ALIWA) prepared to launch the inaugural Nigeria Leadership Dialogue, it needed to establish credibility, build media visibility and introduce the Dialogue as a serious platform for governance and institutional discourse. Despite its affiliation with the Aspen Global Leadership Network, ALIWA had limited existing media visibility and few established press relationships. The task was to differentiate the Dialogue in a crowded conference landscape and reach the stakeholders who shape national leadership conversations.

Our approach

SKOT developed an integrated communications and stakeholder engagement strategy to introduce ALIWA to Nigeria's governance, policy and corporate media ecosystem.

The Dialogue was positioned as a cross-sector leadership intervention focused on governance, systems thinking and institutional integrity, rather than a conventional advocacy event. Structured press engagement and journalist introductions helped establish foundational media relationships.

Stakeholder experience, event curation and visual identity were designed to reflect the seniority of the audience, while executive profiling, post-event editorial content and media amplification extended the conversations beyond the convening.

The impact

The inaugural Nigeria Leadership Dialogue secured coverage across Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3 digital and traditional media platforms. The campaign also established new relationships with journalists and editors and convened public- and private-sector leaders around values-based leadership, institutional accountability and governance reform.

The SKOT contribution

SKOT helped ALIWA introduce the Nigeria Leadership Dialogue as a credible platform for cross-sector leadership and governance conversations.
